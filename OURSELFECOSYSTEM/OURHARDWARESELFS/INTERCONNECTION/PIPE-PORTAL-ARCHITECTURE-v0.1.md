## Canonical Interconnection Architecture v0.1

Status: CANONICAL ARCHITECTURE / CONTRACT IMPLEMENTATION
Authority domains: OURHARDWARESELFS, PIPE, PORTAL, OURKERNELSELF
Continuity relation: LENREK

## Topology

OURHARDWARESELFS
Hardware reality registry · Identity · Jurisdiction · Authority · Admission

↕ governed interconnection

PIPE | PORTAL
PIPE: communication and transport
PORTAL: identity-binding evaluation, admission, capability negotiation, and transition control

↕

OURKERNELSELF

LENREK is orthogonal to this vertical pathway. It is coupled to OURKERNELSELF lifecycle and persistent hardware continuity. It is not a peer authority domain in the passage and does not grant passage admission.

## Authority ownership

### OURHARDWARESELFS
Owns canonical hardware reality records, typed identity, OS and boot observations, jurisdiction declarations, authority boundaries, moat assessments, and hardware admission status. It is the source registry for the identity and jurisdiction claims that a passage request references.

### PIPE
Moves messages and evidence between authorized endpoints. PIPE may frame, route, queue, retry, correlate, and deliver messages within configured transport policy. PIPE cannot create identity, grant admission, expand jurisdiction, authorize execution, or convert a connection into trust.

### PORTAL
Evaluates a proposed transition using registry identity, binding evidence, admission state, requested capability, caller authority, target jurisdiction, freshness, and policy. PORTAL may issue a bounded passage decision or deny/quarantine the request. PORTAL does not create the underlying identity or hardware authority. Its decision is a scoped admission artifact, not a kernel execution grant.

### OURKERNELSELF
Enforces platform and kernel-level controls inside the admitted substrate. It independently evaluates whether a requested operation is permitted by local kernel/OS policy and the authority presented. A PORTAL passage decision is necessary where policy requires it, but is never sufficient by itself to bypass local enforcement.

### LENREK
Persists continuity state, recovery manifests, boot/session evidence, and lifecycle transitions. LENREK may provide evidence to the portal and receive authorized lifecycle events. It does not grant admission, create hardware identity, or keep ordinary kernel computation alive without power.

## Governing invariant

DISCOVERY != ADMISSION != CONNECTION != EXECUTION.

Additional invariants:
- PIPE_CONNECTION != PORTAL_ADMISSION.
- PORTAL_ADMISSION != KERNEL_AUTHORIZATION.
- KERNEL_AUTHORIZATION != ACTUATION.
- ACTUATION != VERIFIED_EFFECT.
- Identity evidence is not created by a transport endpoint.
- A portal decision cannot enlarge the source or target jurisdiction.
- No cross-reality authority inheritance occurs by hostname, route, credential reuse, process ancestry, or model assertion.
- Evidence exchanged over PIPE retains original source, observer, timestamp, digest, and custody metadata.

## Governed passage sequence

1. DISCOVER: identify source and target records; no trust is implied.
2. BIND: resolve typed identity relations and validate current evidence.
3. REQUEST_ADMISSION: submit requested direction, capability, purpose, caller, target, and expiry to PORTAL.
4. EVALUATE: PORTAL checks identity, jurisdiction, moat posture, authority, policy, and evidence freshness.
5. ADMIT_OR_DENY: issue bounded passage decision with explicit scope and expiry; no execution grant.
6. NEGOTIATE: negotiate only capabilities permitted by both jurisdictions and the passage decision.
7. CONNECT: PIPE establishes transport using the approved endpoint and negotiated capability envelope.
8. REQUEST_EXECUTION: caller submits a typed operation request.
9. ENFORCE: OURKERNELSELF performs local authorization and execution-class checks.
10. WITNESS: independent evidence records requested action, decision, execution, and observed effect separately.

A failure at any stage stops progression. A later stage cannot retroactively make an earlier stage valid.

Kernel execution receipt remains independently generated by OURKERNELSELF / its authorized witness. A PIPE connection receipt is not an execution receipt.

## Failure and revocation
- Stale or conflicting identity evidence: DENY or QUARANTINE.
- Unresolved binding: DENY.
- Missing jurisdiction: DENY.
- Missing authority grant: DENY.
- Expired portal decision: disconnect and require reevaluation.
- Capability outside admitted scope: reject before delivery or execution.
- Local kernel policy denial: no execution, regardless of portal admission.
- Revocation: invalidate passage decision and close associated connections where technically supported.
- Evidence loss: mark unverified; never infer success.

## Implementation boundary
This v0.1 package defines the contract and a local assessment validator. It does not replace or silently reconfigure the existing Pipe|PORTAL runtime, create a production transport, issue cryptographic signatures, alter kernel enforcement, or admit a hardware reality.
