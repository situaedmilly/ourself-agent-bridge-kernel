# LENREK Relation to OURKERNELSELF and PIPE|PORTAL

LENREK is the continuity and recovery reality attached to hardware and operating-system lifecycle.

LENREK is not inserted into the vertical authority chain:

OURHARDWARESELFS ↕ PIPE|PORTAL ↕ OURKERNELSELF

LENREK has two governed relations:
1. Substrate/lifecycle relation to OURKERNELSELF: persistent boot identity, state, recovery, shutdown evidence, and re-entry prerequisites.
2. Evidence relation to PORTAL: supplies verified continuity evidence when a passage decision depends on boot or recovery state.

LENREK does not grant passage, establish identity by itself, authorize kernel execution, or confer authority on PIPE.

Power boundary remains explicit: persistent data may survive power loss; active controllers require independent power; ordinary kernel execution stops when power is removed.
